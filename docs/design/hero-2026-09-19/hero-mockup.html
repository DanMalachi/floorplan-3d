done.design · homepage hero · FINAL PICKS 19 Sep 2026: Archivo 800 + Newsreader Italic · Heebo Black + Bona Nova Italic

Round 2: sharper type, a real floorplan, new serifs

  • English headline is now Archivo 800. The rest of the page copy uses Archivo too.
  • Hebrew sans: Rubik is out. Four sharper, heavier options are below.
  • Serifs: a new round, and all of them slant, Hebrew included. Hebrew rarely has true italics, so each Hebrew option is labelled true italic, traditional semi-cursive, or slanted by the browser.
  • Floorplan: redrawn by a stronger model. CAD-style rectangular furniture, no wooden floors, tiles only in wet rooms.
  • The fade: the plan is fully clear under the whole left 44%, so it never sits behind the text.
  • "done." stays "done." in Hebrew, with the dot always on the right. It's isolated as a left-to-right word.

About the Claude/Anthropic look: Anthropic's own serifs are paid, licensed fonts, and the no-paid-assets rule means done. can't ship them. The Latin options below are free fonts (OFL) in the same spirit: literary, calligraphic italics that sit well beside a heavy sans. Newsreader and Source Serif 4 are the closest in feel.
