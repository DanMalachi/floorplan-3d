# Reference image contract

Image-conditioned 3D quality begins with the reference. Reject a weak reference before spending 3D credits.

## Hero image

Create one original product image with:

- exactly one freestanding furniture item, fully visible and centered;
- three-quarter front view at natural product-photography perspective, roughly 50–70 mm equivalent rather than a wide-angle room lens;
- a plain neutral seamless background and soft diffuse light;
- a small honest floor-contact shadow, but no dramatic cast shadows or colored environment light;
- clear negative spaces between legs, under the frame, around arms, and through open shelving;
- believable joinery, thicknesses, cushion volume, seams, piping, hardware, and gravity;
- the exact intended wood species, finish, fabric weave, metal finish, and color;
- no room, props, people, plants, text, watermark, logo, brand mark, or cropped edge.

Avoid vague prompts such as “luxury modern chair.” Resolve a specific design language, construction, materials, proportions, and distinctive-but-original details. “High class” must be visible as restrained proportions, convincing materials, precise edge treatment, and coherent construction—not gold trim, extra ornament, or glossy lighting.

## Hidden geometry

For objects whose back or side materially differs, create consistent side and rear views by editing the approved hero design rather than generating unrelated new images. Match part count, dimensions, materials, seams, hardware, and leg placement. If the provider supports multi-image input, use the consistent set; otherwise keep the extra views as repair and review evidence.

## Preflight score

Before 3D generation, score 1–5 for silhouette, original design, construction plausibility, material clarity, negative-space clarity, and view suitability. Require at least 4 in every category. If a leg, opening, edge, or material boundary is ambiguous in 2D, expect the 3D generator to invent it.

Save the exact accepted image, prompt, seed/tool metadata when available, and rights record in the candidate's `inputs/` directory.
